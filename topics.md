- Build Your Email List Now: The list you build in October is the audience you sell to in November.
- Is Your Theme Outdated?: An old theme limits your design, your speed and what you can edit yourself.

## AI SEO / AEO / GEO

- SEO vs AEO vs GEO: Three terms, one goal: being found wherever people look for answers.
- Get Cited in AI Overviews: Google's AI Overviews answer first and link second. Be one of the sources.
- What Is GEO?: Generative Engine Optimization means making your brand the source AI tools quote.
- Do You Need llms.txt?: llms.txt is a simple text file that points AI tools to your most important pages.
- Answer First. Explain After.: AI tools and busy readers both want the answer before the background.
- Are You Blocking AI Crawlers?: If AI bots cannot read your pages, AI answers cannot cite them.
- E-E-A-T Still Wins: Experience, expertise, authority and trust matter even more in AI search.
- Schema Helps AI Read You: Structured data removes guesswork about who you are and what you offer.
- Mentions Are The New Links: AI tools learn who to recommend from how often trusted sources mention a brand.
- Fewer Clicks, More Answers: More searches now end without a click. Visibility is the new traffic.
- FAQs Built for AEO: A good FAQ section is ready-made material for answer engines.
- Show Up in ChatGPT: ChatGPT searches the web to answer questions. Make your site easy to find and quote.
- Ready for AI Mode?: Google's AI Mode answers complex questions in a conversation, with follow-ups.
- Be a Clear Entity: Search engines and AI need to know exactly who your business is.
- AI Reads Reddit Too: AI answers often draw on community discussions, not only websites.
- Own a Topic, Not a Keyword: Sites that cover a subject fully are trusted more than sites with one good page.
- Original Data Gets Cited: AI tools and writers look for facts they can quote. Give them yours.
- Speed Still Matters: Core Web Vitals are still the base that every other SEO effort stands on.
- Local SEO Meets AI: AI assistants recommend local businesses too. Your profile is your pitch.
- Track Your AI Visibility: You cannot improve what you do not measure, and AI answers can be measured.
- Write in Quotable Passages: AI lifts single passages, not whole pages. Make each one stand alone.
- Product Pages for AI Shopping: AI shopping assistants compare products on facts. Make yours easy to read.
- Keep Content Fresh: Outdated pages lose trust with readers, Google and AI tools alike.
- Internal Links Guide AI: Links between your pages show which ones matter and how topics connect.
- Video Is Search Too: Video results appear in Google and in AI answers. One clear video can do a lot.
- Quality Links Beat Quantity: One link from a relevant, trusted site is worth more than a hundred weak ones.
- Get Cited by Perplexity: Perplexity shows its sources next to every answer. Aim to be one of them.
- Show Who's Behind It: Real people and real credentials make content easier to trust.
- People Ask Full Questions: Searches are getting longer and more conversational. Match how people talk.
- AI SEO Checklist: A quick check to see if your site is ready for AI-driven search.
